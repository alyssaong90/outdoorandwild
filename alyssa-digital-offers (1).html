How I keep AI honest

Three field rules.

  1. Draft with AI, sign off on the ground. Nothing ships that I wouldn't stand behind at a trailhead.
  2. Real data over invented voice. Stories start from actual routes, races and gear, not generic copy.
  3. Safety and trust first. Tools inform decisions; they don't replace guides, coaches or common sense.
